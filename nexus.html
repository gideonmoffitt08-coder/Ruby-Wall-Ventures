What Is Nexus AI?

A seasoned business mentor in your pocket.

Nexus AI is a full-featured AI-powered business advisor platform built and operated by Ruby Wall. Whether you are just starting out, trying to grow your customer base, or scaling your revenue — Nexus AI delivers personalized, actionable advice tailored specifically to your business, your goals, and your current stage.

Unlike a one-size-fits-all chatbot, Nexus AI knows your business from the first conversation and remembers everything going forward.

01

Personalized Business Advising

Every client goes through a quick onboarding process that captures their business type, primary goal, and experience level. From that moment forward, every conversation is tailored to their specific situation — not generic advice pulled from a textbook.

Nexus AI is built specifically for small and local businesses in the Katy and Houston area — the kind of businesses that don't have marketing departments, business coaches, or large budgets.

Nexus AI Advisor
02

Conversation Memory

Nexus AI remembers. After every session, it automatically extracts and saves key facts, decisions, challenges, and action items from the conversation.

When clients return, the advisor already knows who they are, what they have been working on, and where they left off. No re-explaining. No starting over.

  • Automatically extracts key facts after each session
  • Saves decisions, challenges, and action items
  • Context carries forward into every future conversation

03

Chat History

Like ChatGPT or Claude, clients can start new conversations while keeping all past chats accessible in a sidebar. Every conversation is saved, searchable, and organized by date — so nothing gets lost.

  • All conversations saved permanently
  • Organized and searchable by date
  • Start a new chat without losing previous sessions

04

30 & 90-Day Growth Plans

Clients can generate a fully personalized growth plan at any time. Answer two questions — what you want to improve and your timeline — and Nexus AI builds a structured, phase-by-phase action plan with specific tasks tailored to your exact business type and goal.

  • 30-day and 90-day plan options
  • Structured phases with specific tasks
  • Saved permanently with checkable items
  • Up to three plans side by side for comparison
30-Day Growth Plan
05

Action Item Extractor

After any advisory conversation, tap one button and Nexus AI automatically extracts every task, recommendation, and to-do from the chat into a personal checklist. Items can be checked off as completed and cleared when done.

  • One-tap extraction from any conversation
  • Organized personal checklist
  • Check off items as you complete them
  • Clear completed items when ready
Action Checklist
06

Log Progress

Clients can log what they have accomplished at any time. The advisor responds with updated recommendations based on their progress, keeps the momentum going, and adjusts its strategy as the business evolves.

  • Log progress any time, in any session
  • Advisor responds with updated strategy
  • Adaptive recommendations as your business grows
