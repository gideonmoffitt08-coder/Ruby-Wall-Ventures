What Is Meridian?

Professional presentations in minutes, not hours.

Meridian eliminates the need for design skills, expensive software, or hours of manual slide building. It is a production-ready platform with user authentication, tiered subscription plans, and a full presentation experience — accessible directly from any browser.

Feature 01

AI Slide Generation

Describe your business, service, or topic in plain language. Claude AI generates a complete, structured slide deck tailored to your content — headlines, body text, bullet points, quotes, stat slides, and a call-to-action — instantly.

No prompting skills required. No templates to wrestle with. Just describe what you need and Meridian builds the deck.

  • Full deck generation from a single text description
  • Headlines, bullets, quotes, stat slides, and CTAs
  • Structured layout applied automatically
  • Instant output — no wait, no queue

Feature 03

Data Visualization

Paste raw data and Meridian automatically generates the most appropriate chart or visualization, inserted into the deck as dedicated slides.

  • Bar charts for category comparisons
  • Line charts for trends over time
  • Pie and doughnut charts for proportional breakdowns
  • Tables for structured multi-column data
Feature 04

Presentation Mode

A fullscreen Presentation Mode lets you present directly from the browser with 8 animated slide transitions, keyboard navigation, dot indicators, a progress bar, and auto-hiding controls.

Feature 05

Per-Slide Editing

Every element of every slide is editable after generation. Modify headlines, body text, bullet points, stat numbers, and subheadlines directly on the slide. Change background color, swap fonts, and adjust text size.

  • Edit headlines, body, bullets, and stats inline
  • Change background color per slide
  • Swap between 7 professional fonts
  • 4 preset text size scales
