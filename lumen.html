What Is LUMEN?

Not just a caption generator.

LUMEN is a complete content intelligence platform with five specialized tools built specifically for local business owners — restaurants, barbershops, gyms, salons, retail shops, and service businesses — who need to be active on social media but don't have the time, budget, or expertise to hire a full marketing team.

Every tool pulls from your Brand Voice profile automatically. Nothing ever sounds generic.

01

Content Studio

Answer three simple questions about your business and LUMEN generates scroll-stopping social media posts for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok in seconds.

Every post is tailored to your brand voice, your audience, and your platform. Post coaching cards appear beneath every post telling you exactly how to maximize performance — the best time to post, your strongest element, and one specific tweak to make it hit harder.

  • Instagram, Facebook & TikTok ready
  • Brand-voice matched content every time
  • Post coaching card with every generation
  • Optimal posting time recommendations

A ten-question onboarding experience that teaches LUMEN exactly how your business sounds. Once trained, every piece of content LUMEN creates automatically reflects your personality, your audience, and your communication style.

  • 10-question voice onboarding
  • Applied automatically across all five tools
  • Unique to every business
  • Stored locally per user account
